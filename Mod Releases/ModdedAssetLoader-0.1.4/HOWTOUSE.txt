Copy this folder and rename the folder.
Place exported .metem files in AssetBundles folder
Replace icon.png with a unique icon for your mod.
Update content of manifest.json
- Name, short description and version
Update content of README.md
- Update main header: should be name of your mod
- Update description: can be more complex than the one in manifest.json
Rename ModdedAssetLoader.dll

When done, zip the folder.
The zip filder can be uploaded to Thunderstore, or loaded locally by Mod Managers for testing.
